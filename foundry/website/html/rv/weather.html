What this is (and isn't)

  • This is climate normal, not a forecast. Source: Open-Meteo ERA5 reanalysis, daily-minimum temp and daily precipitation averaged over the same calendar dates from 1995–2024 at each stop's coordinates.
  • No real 30-day forecast exists for daily temps at a specific location — public weather models are only reliable out to ~10–14 days.
  • Any individual year can deviate ±5–10°F from these averages. Heat domes happen, and so do unusual monsoon seasons.
  • "Wet day" = ≥0.1 inch precipitation. Most monsoon storms cross that threshold easily.
  • Closer to departure (within ~14 days), check NOAA's actual forecast for the first stops.
  • Mountain stops (Mancos, Ridgway, Crested Butte) will feel cooler than these numbers when you're sleeping at higher elevation than the lat/lon coordinate — coordinates are town center.
