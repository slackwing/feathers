# Verified RV Itinerary (15 days, San Diego → NYC)

24ft Sprinter. Two interchangeable start dates: **Jul 6 → Jul 20, 2026** OR **Jul 16 → Jul 30, 2026**. Same route, same daily plan.

**Key changes from earlier drafts:**
- No Tucson rest day (Andrew didn't ask for it; freed a day)
- Breaking Bad tour + Meow Wolf SF consolidated into a single Day 6 (1 hr drive between, both ~2-3 hr activities)
- Midwest/east split into 4 chiller ~5 hr days instead of 3 punishing 7.5-8 hr days
- Sleep at elevation (BLM / National Forest dispersed) where geography allows

Drive times are conservative RV estimates (cars ~15–25% faster). Mountain segments padded extra.

## Day 1 — Jul 6 (early) / Jul 16 (late) — San Diego
- **Sleep:** Mission Bay / Sunset Cliffs neighborhood.
- **Drive:** 0 mi.
- **Activities:** Fly in, pick up RV, stock + fuel. Easy evening.

## Day 2 — Jul 7 / Jul 17 — San Diego
- **Sleep:** Same.
- **Drive:** 0 mi.
- **Activities:** Sunset Cliffs golden hour, La Jolla tide pools (low tide), breweries (Stone, Ballast Point, AleSmith), Tacos Al Gordo.

## Day 3 — Jul 8 / Jul 18 — Tucson (Gilbert Ray)
- **Sleep:** Gilbert Ray Campground, Tucson Mountain Park (~3,000 ft, shaded — paid, $20/night).
- **Drive:** ~410 mi, ~7 hr. SD → I-8 E → I-10 E. Start early.
- **Activities:** Evening: Gates Pass overlook (Kinney Rd approach if length-restricted). Dinner in RV.

## Day 4 — Jul 9 / Jul 19 — Aguirre Spring BLM
- **Sleep:** Aguirre Spring Campground (BLM, $7, 5,700 ft).
- **Drive:** ~280 mi, ~2.5 hr. Tucson → I-10 E → bypass Las Cruces, climb the Organ Mtns east side.
- **Morning Day 4 (before departure):** Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum (7:30am open, beat heat), Signal Hill Petroglyphs in Saguaro NP West, lunch in Lordsburg NM.
- **Evening:** Spectacular Organ Mountains sunset. Optional dinner detour Old Mesilla.

## Day 5 — Jul 10 / Jul 20 — White Sands → Cloudcroft
- **Sleep:** Cloudcroft / Lincoln NF dispersed off NM-130 (8,600 ft).
- **Drive:** ~110 mi, ~2 hr. Aguirre Spring → US-70 over San Augustin Pass → White Sands → Alamogordo lunch + Boot Hill RV dump → NM-82 climb to Cloudcroft.
- **Activities:** White Sands Dunes Drive 9am-noon, sledding ($17 at Trading Co), Heart of the Dune Field. Leave by 1pm before brutal heat. Dramatic 100°F → 55°F cooldown.

## Day 6 — Jul 11 / Jul 21 — ABQ + Santa Fe (consolidated)
- **Sleep:** Hyde Park Rd / Santa Fe NF dispersed (~10,000 ft, stargazing).
- **Drive:** ~290 mi, ~3 hr driving (plus activity stops). Cloudcroft → US-54 N → I-25 N → ABQ → Santa Fe → NM-475.
- **Morning (ABQ Breaking Bad):** Breakfast burritos in Old Town. BB tour 9am-12pm — Walter's house, Twisters/Los Pollos, A1A car wash, Crossroads Motel. (3 hr, ~4 sites — don't try to do all 19.)
- **Afternoon (Santa Fe Meow Wolf):** 1 hr drive. Meow Wolf — House of Eternal Return 2-3 hr ($32 book ahead). Plaza walk, green chile cheeseburger dinner (Tia Sophia's, Shake Foundation).
- **Evening:** Climb NM-475 to Hyde Park Rd dispersed.

## Day 7 — Jul 12 / Jul 22 — Mancos (Mesa Verde)
- **Sleep:** San Juan NF dispersed near Mancos (FR 561, pine forest).
- **Drive:** ~250 mi, ~5.5 hr. Hyde Park → US-84 N → US-160 W through Pagosa Springs (gas + green chile lunch) and Durango. Sustained 7,000-8,500 ft elevation.
- **Activities:** Arrive late afternoon. Mesa Verde NP Mesa Top Loop at golden hour. Sunset over Montezuma Valley.

## Day 8 — Jul 13 / Jul 23 — Last Dollar Road (Telluride)
- **Sleep:** Last Dollar Rd / Uncompahgre NF dispersed (lower 3 mi accessible to Sprinter on dry gravel — verify recent road conditions).
- **Drive:** ~110 mi, ~3 hr. Mancos → US-160 → US-491 → CO-145 over Lizard Head Pass (10,222 ft) → Telluride → US-550 N → Ridgway.
- **Activities:** Park south end of Telluride and walk into the box canyon. Free gondola up to Mountain Village. Afternoon: drive to Ridgway, soak at Orvis Hot Springs ($30). Up Last Dollar for night.

## Day 9 — Jul 14 / Jul 24 — Cement Creek (Black Canyon + Crested Butte)
- **Sleep:** Cement Creek Rd FR 740 dispersed (Gunnison NF, ~2-6 mi up from CO-135). Creek-side sites in pine.
- **Drive:** ~130 mi, ~3 hr. Last Dollar → Ridgway → US-50 E → CO-92 detour to **Black Canyon North Rim** (open to all RVs, less crowded, arguably better views than South). Then US-50 → CO-135 N to Crested Butte.
- **Activities:** Black Canyon N Rim viewpoints AM, Crested Butte Elk Ave evening (Secret Stash pizza, Teocalli Tamale).

## Day 10 — Jul 15 / Jul 25 — Denver
- **Sleep:** Cherry Creek SP ($41 full hookups — mid-trip water/dump/shower reset).
- **Drive:** ~220 mi, ~4.5 hr. Cement Creek → CO-135 S → US-50 E → US-285 N over Kenosha Pass (10,001 ft).
- **Activities:** Meow Wolf Denver — Convergence Station (2-3 hr, $38). Dinner with friends. RiNo street art if time.

## Day 11 — Jul 16 / Jul 26 — Ogallala NE
- **Sleep:** Lake McConaughy SRA ($8, beach camping on the reservoir).
- **Drive:** ~225 mi, ~3.5 hr. Denver → I-76 E into Nebraska.
- **Activities:** Chiller short day. Afternoon swim, sunset over the water. Recover.

## Day 12 — Jul 17 / Jul 27 — Lincoln · Omaha · Des Moines
- **Sleep:** Des Moines Walmart or Cabela's.
- **Drive:** ~370 mi, ~5 hr driving + 2 activity stops. Ogallala → I-80 E.
- **Activities:** Lincoln AM — thrifting + Sheldon Museum of Art (free, opens 10am). Omaha midday — Joslyn Art Museum (free, closes 4pm) + Riverfront walk. Des Moines evening — dinner along I-80.

## Day 13 — Jul 18 / Jul 28 — South Bend IN
- **Sleep:** South Bend Walmart Supercenter.
- **Drive:** ~380 mi, ~5 hr. Des Moines → I-80 E across IA & IL → skirt Chicago south on I-80 → into Indiana.
- **Activities:** Optional Indiana Dunes leg-stretch on Lake Michigan. Dinner along I-80.

## Day 14 — Jul 19 / Jul 29 — Allentown PA
- **Sleep:** Allentown Walmart Supercenter.
- **Drive:** ~470 mi, ~5 hr. South Bend → I-80 E through OH and mountainous PA.
- **Activities:** Mostly drive day. Dinner at Cracker Barrel along I-80.

## Day 15 — Jul 20 / Jul 30 — Home
- **Sleep:** Home (NYC).
- **Drive:** ~95 mi, ~2.5 hr. Allentown → I-78 E into NJ → into NYC.
- **Activities:** Easy final push, arrive midday. Unpack. Order delivery.

## What changed vs. previous draft (audit trail)

1. **Removed Tucson rest day** — the first planning agent added it; Andrew didn't ask for it.
2. **Consolidated ABQ + Santa Fe into Day 6** — Breaking Bad tour (3 hr) and Meow Wolf SF (2-3 hr) easily fit in one day with the 1 hr drive between, especially when you focus on the 4 best BB sites rather than all 19.
3. **Replaced Lincoln/Iowa City/Cleveland with Ogallala/Des Moines/South Bend/Allentown** — same total distance, but split into 4 manageable 5 hr days instead of 3 punishing 7.5/8/7.5 hr days. Activity content (Sheldon, Joslyn) moves to Day 12 as morning/midday stops.
4. **Added Ogallala chiller day** — recovers from Colorado descent, sets up the museum sweep day with energy.
5. **Sleep coords throughout shifted to BLM/NF dispersed** at elevation where the philosophy works (Days 4-9). Walmart/state-park lots east of Denver where it doesn't.
