## Notes on the new structure

- **Day 4 Gila Bend** is the only motel on the trip. Cost: ~$120/night. Worth it: Day 4 Gila Bend nightly low averages 83°F outside (≈95°F inside the RV) — that's the trip's worst sleep night by far, and the SD→Tucson alternative is a brutal 7-hour day.
- **Day 5 is dense:** finish 3.5hr drive to Tucson, 4-5hr of activities, then 1hr push to Willcox. Start early from Gila Bend.
- **Day 6 still does White Sands** but as a morning stop on the drive Willcox → Cloudcroft. Need to wake at ~6am to beat the heat. ~3 hr drive to White Sands.
- **Day 14 final drive (9.5 hr)** is the trip's longest. Pre-plan fuel and food stops. Plan to leave South Bend by 7am to be home by 6pm.
