**Vehicle:** 2018 Mercedes Benz Sprinter 2500 Extended (24ft × 7.7ft)
**Dates:** Jul 5–20, 2026 (early) OR Jul 16–31, 2026 (late). Same daily structure.
**Philosophy:** Decouple sleeping from activities. Sleep on free public land, BLM dispersed, or NF dispersed between destinations whenever heat + geography allow. Wake "in the middle of nowhere," drive 1–2 hr to breakfast town, drive another 1–2 hr to the day's main activity, do activity, drive again in the evening to next sleep spot — dinner along the way.

This review walks the trip day by day, names specific sleep spots, calls out where the model breaks (cities, NPs, eastern half), and lays out a water/dump/shower rhythm. Where I name a specific FR or pullout, that's based on regional knowledge — verify on iOverlander/Campendium/FreeCampsites.net before relying on it for a 24ft Sprinter.

## Day 1 — Sun Jul 5 / Jul 16 — Fly into San Diego

**Sleep:** Pacific Beach area, San Diego (~32.7975, -117.2370). Specific spot TBD (street parking near OB/PB, or Mission Bay campground).

**Afternoon/evening flow:** Alaska Airlines 11:30 AM → SAN 2:27 PM. Pick up the RV near North Park, refuel. Drive to Coronado for water refill station. Head to **Musita Abyssinian Bistro** in University Heights for early dinner (opens 5 PM Sun — RV parking: drive west on Madison Ave, cars get sparser). Then **Sunset Cliffs** for the 8 PM sunset. Optional shower at Planet Fitness Scranton (9420 Scranton Rd) or try the RV shower. End up in Pacific Beach.

**Philosophy break:** Yes — city night, no public land near the coast. Pacific Beach is the right pay-for-it choice.

## Day 2 — Mon Jul 6 / Jul 17 — San Diego (second night)

**Sleep:** Same Pacific Beach area.

**Morning flow:** **La Jolla tide pools** at 7:15 AM (low tide 0.8 ft at 7:52 AM — peak pooling). Breakfast/brunch after.

**Afternoon/evening flow:** Free day. Shopping for trip supplies, Amazon locker pickups. Optional **brewery + Shout House** evening — take the train (Coaster/Trolley) so the RV doesn't have to move.

**Philosophy break:** Same — still a city night, deliberately so.

**Water/dump:** Trip starts here, so tanks should be clean/full from the start. Mission Bay-area campgrounds offer full hookups.

## Day 3 — Jul 8 / Jul 18 — SD → Tucson

**Sleep:** Gilbert Ray Campground, Tucson Mountain Park (32.2486, -111.1647). $20/night, ~3,000 ft, shaded sites.

**Morning flow:** Leave SD by 7am to beat desert heat. Lunch at Yuma Walmart or push to Lordsburg. 7 hr drive total via I-8 → I-10.

**Evening flow:** Arrive mid-afternoon. Drive over **Gates Pass** for sunset overlook (approach from Kinney Rd if length-restriction signs flag the 24ft Sprinter). Dinner in RV at camp, early bed.

**Philosophy break:** Yes — Tucson in July is genuinely dangerous for low-desert dispersed sleeping (Snyder Hill BLM hits 110°F at night). Gilbert Ray's slight elevation + Tucson Mountain Park shade = the safe choice. This is one of the trip's necessary "pay for it" nights.

**Alternative if heat tolerance is higher than expected:** Coronado NF dispersed on Mt. Lemmon at 8,000 ft (cool nights) — but verify FR access for 24ft; upper sections get rough.

---

## Day 4 — Jul 9 / Jul 19 — Tucson AM → Aguirre Spring BLM

**Sleep:** Aguirre Spring Campground (32.378, -106.563). BLM, $7/night, 5,700 ft, east side of the Organ Mountains. Dramatic sunset/dawn views of the dunes from the pass.

**Morning flow:** Wake at Gilbert Ray. **Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum** opens 7:30am — be there at open (the activity itself is 3-4 hr but mornings beat the 100°F+ midday). Quick stop at **Signal Hill Petroglyphs** in Saguaro NP West on the way out.

**Drive:** Lunch in Lordsburg NM Walmart (mid-day stop), then push 2.5 hr to Aguirre Spring. Arrive mid-afternoon.

**Evening flow:** Naps + early dinner at camp. Optional Old Mesilla green chile detour in Las Cruces if energy allows.

**Philosophy break:** None — this is the textbook execution. BLM dispersed-adjacent for $7, positioned for an early White Sands tomorrow.

**Water/dump:** Aguirre Spring has water (verify season — sometimes off in extreme heat) but no dump. Plan dump at Boot Hill RV Alamogordo tomorrow.

---

## Day 5 — Jul 10 / Jul 20 — White Sands AM → Cloudcroft NF

**Sleep:** Cloudcroft / Lincoln National Forest dispersed off NM-130 (32.9569, -105.743). 8,600 ft, pine forest, free. Specific area: **Sleepygrass Campground vicinity / FR 24** has well-documented dispersed sites.

**Morning flow:** Wake at Aguirre Spring → coffee in Las Cruces (Andele's) → 1 hr to **White Sands NP**. Arrive 9am. Dunes Drive + sledding + Heart of the Dune Field, leave by 1pm before brutal heat.

**Evening flow:** Lunch in Alamogordo (Pizza Patio), dump tanks at **Boot Hill RV** (~$10-15 dump-only). Climb US-82 to Cloudcroft (45 min, gorgeous switchbacks, RV-manageable). Sleep at 8,600 ft pines — **dramatic 100°F → 55°F cooldown**.

**Philosophy break:** None. This is the trip's first textbook "wake at one dispersed site, drive to main activity, drive to next dispersed site" day.

**Water/dump:** Boot Hill RV dump done. Tanks reset for the dispersed run.

---

## Day 6 — Jul 11 / Jul 21 — Cloudcroft → ABQ + Santa Fe (consolidated) → Hyde Park Rd

**Sleep:** Hyde Park Road / Santa Fe National Forest dispersed (~35.78, -105.82). Climbs from city to ~10,000 ft over 16 miles on NM-475. Dispersed allowed along FRs branching off. Cool, pine forest, stargazing.

**Morning flow:** Wake at Cloudcroft (cold pine morning!). Coffee in Cloudcroft village → drive 3.5 hr via US-54 N → US-380 W → I-25 N → arrive ABQ Old Town by 10am. **Breaking Bad self-guided tour** — focus on 4-5 best sites (3 hr): Walter's house (3828 Piermont Dr NE), Los Pollos Hermanos / Twisters (4257 Isleta Blvd SW), A1A Car Wash, Crossroads Motel. Skip the comprehensive 19-stop list; that bogs the day down.

**Midday:** Quick breakfast burritos in Old Town, then ABQ → Santa Fe 1 hr.

**Afternoon flow:** **Meow Wolf — House of Eternal Return** 2-3 hr, $32 (book ahead). Plaza walk + Canyon Road galleries if time.

**Evening flow:** Green chile cheeseburger dinner (Tia Sophia's or Shake Foundation). Climb NM-475 Hyde Park Rd for night. Stargazing at 10,000 ft.

**Philosophy break:** Partial — they drive *into* two cities for activities, which violates strict "sleep middle of nowhere, drive to activity." But sleeping above ABQ at Cloudcroft and above Santa Fe at Hyde Park is a strong compromise. The consolidated day is also a big efficiency win (saves a whole calendar day vs. doing each separately).

**Water/dump:** Day 6 is the 5-6 day mark since Mission Bay. Tanks getting full. Consider hitting **Trailer Ranch RV Park** in Santa Fe for a dump-only stop ($15 non-guest) before climbing Hyde Park. Otherwise plan dump for Day 10 Denver.

## Day 7 — Jul 12 / Jul 22 — Hyde Park → Mancos (Mesa Verde)

**Sleep:** San Juan National Forest dispersed near Mancos along **FR 561** and the Transfer Park area (~37.42, -108.20). Pine forest, accessible to a Sprinter on dry forest road.

**Alternative:** **McElmo Canyon Road / Sand Canyon BLM** (~37.36, -108.78) — sandstone country, dark skies, slightly further from Mesa Verde.

**Morning flow:** Wake at Hyde Park Rd alpine. Coffee in Santa Fe (Iconik or Sky Coffee). Drive 5.5 hr via US-84 N → US-160 W through Pagosa Springs (gas + green chile lunch — Kip's Grill is iconic) and Durango. Sustained 7,000-8,500 ft elevation, slow grades for the RV.

**Evening flow:** Arrive Mesa Verde area by late afternoon. Run into the park for **Mesa Top Loop Road** at golden hour — Sun Temple, cliff dwelling overlooks. Sunset over Montezuma Valley. Drive 30 min to FR 561 dispersed for the night.

**Philosophy break:** None — strong fit. NF dispersed at proper trip rhythm.

**Note on Mesa Verde tours:** If they want a ranger-guided **Cliff Palace** or **Balcony House** tour, book at recreation.gov when tickets release (~Jul 4). The Mesa Top Loop doesn't require a tour.

## Day 8 — Jul 13 / Jul 23 — Mancos → Telluride/Ridgway → Last Dollar Rd

**Sleep:** **Last Dollar Road (FR 638)** between Telluride and Ridgway (~37.99, -107.92). High-elevation Uncompahgre NF dispersed with epic views of the Sneffels Range. **Lower 3 miles are accessible to a 2WD Sprinter on dry gravel; upper sections need 4WD.** Verify recent road condition — washboards after rains.

**Alternative:** **Owl Creek Pass Road / Cimarron** east of Ridgway off US-550 (~38.18, -107.55). More accessible, less spectacular.

**Morning flow:** Wake near Mesa Verde → breakfast in Cortez (Spruce Tree Coffee) or Mancos (Absolute Bakery). Drive 3 hr via US-160 → US-491 → CO-145 over **Lizard Head Pass (10,222 ft)** to Telluride. Slow in the RV, budget time.

**Activities:** Park at south end of Telluride, walk into the box canyon (main street ends at a waterfall view). Free gondola up to Mountain Village. 1 hr drive to Ridgway, soak at **Orvis Hot Springs** ($30, worth it).

**Evening flow:** Drive 20 min up Last Dollar Road. ~42°F nights at this elevation — pack blankets, not AC.

**Philosophy break:** None — this is the best dispersed day of the trip.

## Day 9 — Jul 14 / Jul 24 — Last Dollar → Black Canyon → Crested Butte → Cement Creek

**Sleep:** **Cement Creek Road FR 740 dispersed** (Gunnison NF, ~2-6 miles up from CO-135 north of Gunnison, ~38.81, -106.85). Pine forest, creek-side sites, well-established. Confirmed Sprinter-friendly.

**Morning flow:** Wake on Last Dollar Rd (alpine sunrise). Drive 30 min to Ridgway → breakfast at Kate's Place. Drive 2 hr via US-50 E with the **Black Canyon North Rim detour via CO-92 from Hotchkiss** (open to all vehicles — the 22 ft limit is only on the **South Rim Drive**, which they're skipping). North Rim is less crowded and arguably better views. Allow 2 hr for the detour + viewpoints.

**Evening flow:** Lunch in Crested Butte (Secret Stash for pizza, Teocalli Tamale for tacos). Wander Elk Ave — old mining town main street, wildflower season. Drive 30 min back south to Cement Creek dispersed.

**Philosophy break:** None.

**Water/dump:** Day 9 is the 8-day mark since last hookup. Tanks tight. Consider hitting dump/water at **Gunnison KOA** ($15 non-guest) or **Almont Resort** before heading up Cement Creek. Otherwise plan dump for Day 10 Denver (Cherry Creek SP, full hookups).

## Day 10 — Jul 15 / Jul 25 — Cement Creek → Denver

**Sleep:** **Cherry Creek State Park** ($41/night, full hookups). Close-in to Denver, easy in/out for Meow Wolf + friends-dinner.

**Morning flow:** Wake at Cement Creek (creek-side mountain dawn). Coffee in Crested Butte (Camp 4 Coffee). Drive 4.5 hr via CO-135 S → US-50 E to Salida → US-285 N over **Kenosha Pass (10,001 ft)** into Denver. Scenic route; alternative via Monarch Pass + I-70 is similar time.

**Evening flow:** **Meow Wolf Denver — Convergence Station** 2-3 hr (book ahead). Dinner with Andrew's friends. RiNo for street art if time.

**Philosophy break:** Yes, but intentional — friends + Meow Wolf require being in the city. This is the planned "civilization" stop and mid-trip hookup reset.

**Water/dump:** **YES — this is the mid-trip hookup night.** Showers, water tank fill, dump. Charge all devices. Tanks reset.

---

## Day 11 — Jul 16 / Jul 26 — Denver → Ogallala (Lake McConaughy)

**Sleep:** **Lake McConaughy State Recreation Area** (~41.23, -101.74). Nebraska state recreation area, ~$8/night beach camping on the reservoir. RV-friendly. This is a notable upgrade from a Walmart night — they're actually camping near water.

**Morning flow:** Wake at Cherry Creek SP (full hookups + showers — appreciate this). Coffee in Denver. Drive 3.5 hr via I-76 E across NE Colorado into Nebraska. Short chiller day after Colorado.

**Evening flow:** Arrive Lake McConaughy mid-afternoon. Afternoon swim if hot, sunset over the reservoir. Recover from the Rockies-to-plains transition. This is the deliberate "breathing room" day Andrew asked for.

**Philosophy break:** None — a real public-land overnight, not a Walmart. Best Nebraska sleep option you'll find on I-76/I-80.

**Alternative for budget:** Pawnee National Grassland north of I-76 near Sterling (~40.78, -103.40) — free USFS grassland dispersed. Beautiful but more remote; Lake McConaughy is the easier call.

---

## Day 12 — Jul 17 / Jul 27 — Ogallala → Lincoln → Omaha → Des Moines

**Sleep:** **Des Moines Walmart Supercenter** or Cabela's. BLM is long gone east of the Front Range; Walmart is the philosophy translated to the plains.

**Morning flow:** Wake at Lake McConaughy → coffee at camp. Drive 3 hr east to Lincoln. **Lincoln AM:** thrifting (downtown has solid spots) + **Sheldon Museum of Art** (free, on UNL campus, opens 10am). Quick lunch in Haymarket District.

**Midday flow:** Drive 1 hr to Omaha. **Joslyn Art Museum** (free, closes 4pm, reopened 2024 with expansion) + Riverfront walk (Gene Leahy Mall + pedestrian bridge into Iowa).

**Evening flow:** Drive 2.5 hr to Des Moines. Dinner along I-80 (truck stop, Cracker Barrel). Walmart for the night.

**Philosophy break:** Partial — this day stacks two cities (Lincoln + Omaha) plus driving, then ends at a Walmart. It's the right call for the plains — there's no good public land here.

**Water/dump:** Day 12 is 2 days since Cherry Creek hookup. Tanks fine.

---

## Day 13 — Jul 18 / Jul 28 — Des Moines → South Bend

**Sleep:** **South Bend Walmart Supercenter** in Indiana. Solid, RV-friendly, on the I-80 corridor.

**Morning flow:** Wake at Des Moines Walmart → coffee at Cracker Barrel or truck stop. Drive 5 hr east on I-80 across IA & IL. Skirt **Chicago south on I-80** (the bypass — avoids downtown traffic, RV-friendly).

**Midday option:** Pull off at **Indiana Dunes State Park / National Park** for a Lake Michigan leg-stretch (Mt. Baldy parking lot, easy 5 min walk to the lake).

**Evening flow:** Arrive South Bend by evening. Dinner along I-80 (Cracker Barrel = backup overnight option too).

**Philosophy break:** Heavy. This is pure transit through the Midwest farm belt. Accept the Walmart night; don't fight it.

**Water/dump:** Tanks getting tight (3 days since Cherry Creek). If there's a nearby state park or COE campground with dump, hit it. Otherwise push through and dump at home on Day 15.

## Day 14 — Jul 19 / Jul 29 — South Bend → Allentown PA

**Sleep:** **Allentown PA Walmart Supercenter**. Last night out before home.

**Morning flow:** Wake at South Bend Walmart → coffee. Drive 5 hr through OH and mountainous PA. Optional pull-off at the **Ohio Turnpike Service Plaza** (Vermilion Valley, Erie Islands) for a stretch — these are explicitly RV-friendly and also work as overnight backups.

**Evening flow:** Dinner at Cracker Barrel along I-80 PA. Allentown Walmart for the night.

**Philosophy break:** Heavy, but the chiller 5 hr pacing makes this manageable instead of brutal. Andrew specifically asked for shorter days here.

That's roughly every 3-5 days, with the Cherry Creek hookup at the mid-trip pivot point doing the heavy lifting.

### Where the philosophy breaks down

1. **SD Days 1–2** — flying in, no public land near coast. Pay for Mission Bay.
2. **Tucson Day 3** — July low-desert dispersed is unsafe. Pay for Gilbert Ray shade.
3. **Day 6 ABQ + SF** — activities are in-city; sleeping above both at Cloudcroft (the night before) and Hyde Park (after) is the compromise.
4. **Denver Day 10** — friends + Meow Wolf + mid-trip hookups all converge. Pay for Cherry Creek SP.
5. **Days 12–14 plains/midwest** — BLM ends at the Front Range. Walmart, Cracker Barrel, COE lakes, OH Turnpike plazas. "Middle of nowhere" doesn't really exist on I-80 east of Nebraska. The 5 hr chiller days make this manageable.

### Where the consolidations paid off

- **No Tucson rest day** — first planning agent had added it. Cutting it freed a day that gave us the chiller midwest pacing.
- **BB tour + Meow Wolf SF Day 6** — the trip's single biggest day-saver. 1 hr drive between, focused BB tour (4 sites not 19) + 2-3 hr Meow Wolf fits comfortably in one day with elevation sleep on either end.
- **Midwest 4-day split** — old: Lincoln/Iowa City/Cleveland over 3 days = 7.5 + 8 + 7.5 = 23 hr. New: Ogallala/Des Moines/South Bend/Allentown over 4 days + 2.5 hr final = 21 hr but spread to ~5 hr/day. Lincoln/Omaha museum content moved to Day 12 as morning/midday stops.

### Quick-hit "verify before committing"

- **Last Dollar Road FR 638** (Day 8) — lower 3 mi reliably accessible to Sprinter dry, upper 4WD. Check recent reports.
- **Cement Creek Rd FR 740** (Day 9) — established Gunnison NF dispersed, generally good. 2-6 mi up has the best sites.
- **Cloudcroft FR 24** (Day 5) — confirmed Lincoln NF dispersed, verify FR access for 24ft Sprinter on the actual side roads.
- **Hyde Park Rd / NM-475** (Day 6) — NM-475 itself is paved and Sprinter-friendly; some side FRs above Santa Fe are restricted to designated sites only.
- **San Juan NF FR 561** (Day 7) — well-known dispersed area near Mancos, verify conditions.
- **Aguirre Spring water availability** — sometimes off in extreme summer heat. Have a backup plan to fill at Las Cruces.
- **Lake McConaughy SRA** — beach camping varies by water level and season. Verify open and accessible.

### Final notes

- **5 straight nights of free public-land dispersed sleeping** (Days 5–9, Cloudcroft → Hyde Park → Mancos → Last Dollar → Cement Creek) is the dream stretch. The elevation drops sleep temps 10–15°F vs. nearby town stays.
- **July monsoon caveat:** The high-elevation NM stops (Cloudcroft, Hyde Park) hit 40-50% wet-day probability on the late start. Dirt-road dispersed access can degrade after storms. Have a backup paid option in pocket (Santa Fe Skies, Boot Hill RV).
- **The dream stretch (Days 5-9) is also why the early Jul 6 start matters most** — those are exactly the days where 6% vs 13% wet-day probability hits hardest. Drier early-July = better access to the spots we're paying $0 for.
